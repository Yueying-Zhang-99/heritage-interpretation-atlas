ABOUT THE ATLAS 01 / RESEARCH CONTEXT

A living map of
heritage interpretation.

Heritage Interpretation Knowledge Atlas is an evolving research database that maps the theoretical development of heritage interpretation, from monument-centred conservation to plural, participatory and technology-mediated interpretation.

DOCTORAL RESEARCH

面向历史场所多元叙事的公众阐释设计研究
—— 基于游戏化与混合现实

The atlas forms part of doctoral research on public interpretation design for plural narratives of historic places through gamification and mixed reality.
